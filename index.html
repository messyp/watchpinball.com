Made for a small screen

  • The camera does the hard part. It follows the ball and drops to the flippers before it gets there, so you never lose a ball you couldn't see.
  • A second chance. Kickbacks in both outlanes save you from the worst drains.
  • A proper dot-matrix display. It keeps score, counts your bonus and shouts about jackpots.
  • Haptics you can feel. Bumpers thump, and the plunger ratchets as you wind it.

Three moves. All on your wrist.

Turn the Digital Crown down to wind the plunger, then let go to fire.

Tap the left or right of the screen, or give the Digital Crown a flick.

Shake your wrist to nudge the table. Three quick shakes is a tilt.

How to Play teaches you on a real table in about a minute.

A whole arcade in about 3 MB

Every table is drawn in code and every sound is synthesised. Apart from its icon, the game has no image files at all. The five gameplay clips on this page add up to more than three times the size of the game.
